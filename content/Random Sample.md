### Definition
Let $X_{1},..., X_{n}$ be [[Independence of random variables|independent]] random variables that have the same [[Marginal distribution|marginal distribution]] with pdf $f(x)$
This set of random variable $\{X_{1},..., X_{n}\}$ is called a **random sample** from $f(x)$
### Interpretation
- $X_{1},...,X_{n}$ are **independently identically distributed (iid)** with common pdf $f(x)$
- All the random variables will have common [[Population mean|mean]] and [[Population variance|variance]] *e.g.* $E(X_{i})=\mu$ and $\text{Var}(X_{i}) = \sigma^{2}$
