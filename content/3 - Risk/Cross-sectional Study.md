### Concept
- *What is it:* a study that measures exposure and outcome at the same time *i.e.* a snapshot with no follow-up
- *How to measure it:* prevalence
- *Example:* Survey adults in a city and simultaneously record smoking status and the presence of chronic cough.
- *Pros:* 
	- Quick 
	- Inexpensive
- *Cons:*
	- Cannot establish temporal sequence *e.g.* If smokers are found to have more chronic cough, we cannot determine from a cross-sectional study whether smoking preceded the cough, or the cough influenced smoking behaviour
- *When to use it:* estimating disease prevalence or assessing population health at a specific point in time
