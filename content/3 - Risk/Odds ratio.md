### Concept
- *What it is:* the ratio of the **odds** of an outcome occurring in the exposed group to the odds of the same outcome in the unexposed group
	- Odds are probability of event / probability of event not happening
### Definition
![[Risk difference - definition.png|478]]
$$
\begin{align*} \omega &= \frac{O(+)}{O(-)} \\ &= \frac{\frac{\operatorname{Pr}(Disease+|Exposure+)}{\operatorname{Pr}(Disease-|Exposure+)}}{\frac{\operatorname{Pr}(Disease+|Exposure-)}{\operatorname{Pr}(Disease-|Exposure-)}} \\ &= \frac{p_{11} p_{22}}{p_{12} p_{21}} \end{align*}
$$
