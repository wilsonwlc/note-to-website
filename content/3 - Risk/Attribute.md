### Concept
- *What is it:* a [[Risk factor|risk factor]] that is an intrinsic characteristic of the individual (or organism) under study
- *Example:* age, sex, ethnicity, genetic factors, or family history
- *Which:* a subset of characteristics (specifically the intrinsic personal ones)
### Practice
- They are generally fixed or change only slowly and are not the result of an external contact in the usual sense
