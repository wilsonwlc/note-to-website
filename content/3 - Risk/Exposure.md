### Concept
- *What is exposure:* contact with (or possession of) a factor that may influence a health outcome.
- _What is exposure to a risk factor:_ the presence of, or contact with, a [[Risk factor|a risk factor]] before the occurrence of the disease or outcome of interest
- *Example:* 
	- *External:* chemical, infectious agent, radiation, occupational hazard
	- *Behavioural:* smoking, diet, physical activity
### Practice
- Exposure may be measured as a binary variable (*e.g.* smoker/non-smoker). Many exposures are naturally quantitative variables (*e.g.* number of cigarettes smoked per day, alcohol consumption per week, or daily coffee intake)
- For descriptive analyses, continuous exposure variables are sometimes categorised into binary or ordinal groups and summarised using contingency tables. However, continuous exposure variables are often retained in their original form and analysed directly using statistical models to avoid loss of information
