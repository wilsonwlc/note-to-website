### Concept
- Aka **risk ratio**
- *What is it:* the ratio of the [[Risk|risk]] (probability) of an outcome in the exposed group to the risk in the unexposed (or control) group
- *When we need it:* a comparative measure of association in cohort studies
### Definition
![[Risk difference - definition.png|420]]
$$
\begin{align*} \rho &= \frac{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+|Exposure+)}{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+|Exposure-)} \\ &= \frac{\frac{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+ \; \cap \; Exposure +)}{\mathrm{Pr}(Exposure+)}}{\frac{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+ \; \cap \;  Exposure -)}{\mathrm{Pr}(Exposure-)}}\\ &= \frac{\frac{p_{11}}{p_{1+}}}{\frac{p_{21}}{p_{2+}}} \end{align*}
$$
### Interpretation
- It quantifies how many times higher (or lower) the probability of the event is in the exposed group over a specified time period
