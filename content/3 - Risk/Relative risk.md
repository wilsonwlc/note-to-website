### Concept
- Aka **risk ratio**
- *What is it:* the ratio of the risk (probability) of an outcome in the exposed group to the risk in the unexposed (or control) group
- *When we need it:* 
	- Parameter of interest in the studies of risk
	- Predict the occurrence of disease of an individual patient with known exposure history, especially when several risk factors are identified and studied
### Definition
![[Risk difference - definition.png|417]]
$$
\begin{align*} \rho &= \frac{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+|Exposure+)}{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+|Exposure-)} \\ &= \frac{\frac{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+ \; \cap \; Exposure +)}{\mathrm{Pr}(Exposure+)}}{\frac{\mathrm{Pr}(Disease+ \; \cap \;  Exposure -)}{\mathrm{Pr}(Exposure-)}}\\ &= \frac{\frac{p_{11}}{p_{1+}}}{\frac{p_{21}}{p_{2+}}} \end{align*}
$$
