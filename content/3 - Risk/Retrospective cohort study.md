### Concept
- *What is it:* a [[Cohort study|cohort study]] in which researchers identify the exposed and unexposed participants using historical information and obtain outcome data that have already been collected and recorded in existing records
- *Example:* In 2026, obtain employment records from 1980. Identify workers exposed and unexposed to asbestos. Use medical records to determine who developed lung cancer by 2020.
- *Pros:*
	- Faster than prospective cohort studies
	- Less expensive
- *Cons:*
	- Dependent on quality of existing records
	- Important variables may be unavailable or inaccurately recorded
