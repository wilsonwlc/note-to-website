### Concept
- *What is it:* a distinguishing feature, quality, or property of a person, place, or time
- *Example:* 
	- Person: age, sex, race/ethnicity, socioeconomic status, occupation, marital status
	- Place: geographic location, urban/rural, climate
	- Time: season, secular trends, epidemic curves
- *Caution:* Characteristics may or may not be [[Risk factor|risk factors]]
