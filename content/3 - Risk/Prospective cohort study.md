### Concept
- *What is it:* A [[Cohort study|cohort study]] in which researchers identify exposed and unexposed participants and follow the participants and collect new outcome data during the follow-up period
- *Example:* Recruit smokers and non-smokers. Follow them for 10 years and observe their lung cancer occurrence
- *Pros:*
	- Clear temporal relationship between exposure and outcome
	- Less susceptible to recall bias than retrospective designs
- *Cons:* 
	- Expensive 
	- Time-consuming
### Comparison
#### Prospective cohort vs Retrospective cohort
![[Prospective study - Comparison - Retrospective study, Case-control, Clinical Trial.jpg|479]]
- *Similarity:* In both [[Prospective cohort study|prospective]] and [[Retrospective cohort study|retrospective]] cohort studies, researchers can use existing medical records to identify patients and establish their exposure status
- *Difference:* The important distinction is 
	- *Timing of outcome data collection:* whether they obtain outcome information through subsequent follow-up or retrieve outcome information that already exists
	- *Nature of the outcome data:* Whether new outcome information is collected or existing outcome information is retrieved
#### Prospective vs Retrospective vs Ambidirectional
##### Time order
- Research initiation: The date when the research investigation actually begins
- Cohort identification: The time when researchers identify or select the participants to be included in the research
- Follow-up begins: The time when observation of the participants for the outcomes of interest starts
- Outcomes occur: The dates when participants actually experience the outcomes being studied
- Outcome data collection: The time when information about the outcomes is collected or recorded. This may happen when the outcomes occur or later
- Historical formation of the cohort: The time when the participants originally entered the cohort in the past, for example, when they met the eligibility criteria or their exposure status was established
[[Prospective cohort study|Prospective cohort study]]
- Research initiation → Cohort identification → Follow-up begins → Outcomes occur → Outcome data collection
- Cohort identification → Research initiation → Follow-up begins → Outcomes occur → Outcome data collection
[[Retrospective cohort study|Retrospective cohort study]]
- Historical cohort formation → Follow-up begins → Outcomes occur and are recorded → Research initiation → Cohort identification
[[Ambidirectional cohort study|Ambidirectional cohort study]]
- Historical cohort formation → Follow-up begins → Historical outcomes occur and are recorded → Research initiation → Cohort identification → Follow-up continues → New outcomes occur → New outcome data collection
