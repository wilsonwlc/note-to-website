### Concept
- *What is it:* an experimental design in which two or more **factors** (*e.g.* interventions, treatments, or experimental variables) are studied simultaneously, and participants are allocated to all possible combinations of the factor levels
- *What analysis it need:* 
	- For continuous outcome, [[Two-way ANOVA|2-way ANOVA]]
