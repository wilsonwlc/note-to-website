### Concept
- *What is it:* the natural logarithm of the [[Moment generating function (mgf)|moment generating function]]  
### Definition
Let $X$ be a random variable whose moment generating function $M(t)$ exists in a neighbourhood of $t=0$
The cumulant-generating function is
$$\begin{align*} K(t) &= \log M(t) = \log E[e^{tX}] \\ &= \sum\limits_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{\kappa_{r} t^{r}}{r!} = \kappa_{1} \frac{t}{1!} + \kappa_{2} \frac{t^{2}}{2!} + \kappa_{3} \frac{t^{3}}{3!} + \dots \end{align*} $$
- $\kappa_{r}$: [[Cumulant|cumulants]]
