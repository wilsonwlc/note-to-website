### Concept
- *What is it:* The probability distribution of a statistic (viewed as a [[Random variable|random variable]]) when that statistic is computed from [[Random sample|random samples]] of fixed size $n$ drawn from the same population
### Interpretation
1. Start with a well-defined population
2. Consider all possible random samples of the same fixed size $n$
3. From each sample, compute the same statistic *e.g.* the [[Sample mean|sample mean]]
4. The probability distribution of the resulting statistic values is the sampling distribution of that statistic
### Practice
- We almost never enumerate every possible sample. Instead, we 
	- Derive the exact sampling distribution mathematically when possible
	- Invoke asymptotic results *e.g.* **Central Limit Theorem**
	- Approximate the sampling distribution by simulation *e.g.* **Monte-Carlo simulation** or **bootstrap methods**
