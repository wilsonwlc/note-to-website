### Theorem
Suppose $W_{1}, W_{2},...,W_{k}$ are [[Independence of random variables|independent]] random variables such that $\frac{r_i W_i}{E(W_i)} \sim \chi^2_{r_i}$ for $i=1,2,...,k$
Let $W = \sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i} W_{i}$ be a linear combination of $W_{i}$'s. 
To have the approximated distribution $\frac{\nu W}{E(W)} \sim \chi^2_{\nu}$ reasonable, the degrees of freedom $\nu$ is approximated by 
$$
\nu \approx \frac{\left(\sum_{i=1}^{k} a_{i} W_{i}\right)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{k} \frac{a_{i}^{2}W_{i}^{2}}{r_{i}}}
$$
