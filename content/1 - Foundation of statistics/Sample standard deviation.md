### Concept
- *What is it:* non-negative square root of the [[Sample variance|sample variance]]
### Definition
$$
S = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_{i} - \overline{X})^{2}}
$$
- $n$: number of values in the sample
- $X_{i}$: each individual value in the sample
- $\overline{X}$: [[Sample mean|sample mean]]
