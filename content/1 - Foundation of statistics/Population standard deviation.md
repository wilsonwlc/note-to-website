### Definition
The **population standard deviation** of a [[Random variable|random variable]] $X$ is defined as 
$$
\sigma=\sqrt{\text{Var}(X)}
$$
For a finite population of size $N$, 
$$
\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum\limits_{i=1}^{N} (x_{i}-\mu)^2}
$$
- $x_{i}$: each individual value in the population
- $\mu$: [[Population mean|population mean]]
### Interpretation
- It is a measure of spread
