### Concept
- Stand for **Uniformly Minimum-Variance Unbiased Estimator**
- *What is it:* an [[Bias|unbiased]] [[Point estimation|estimator]] has the minimum [[Population variance|variance]] among all unbiased estimators
### Caution
- An UMVUE may not be an [[Efficiency|efficient]] estimator. *Reason:* the [[Cramér-Rao Inequality|Cramér-Rao Lower Bound]] may not be attainable.
