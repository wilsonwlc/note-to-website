### Definition
The **population mean** of a random variable $X$ is defined as the [[Expectation|expected value]] *i.e.* 
$$
\mu=E(X)
$$

For a finite population of size $N$, 
$$
\mu = \frac{1}{N} \sum\limits_{i=1}^{N} x_{i}
$$
- $x_{i}$: the fixed values that constitute the population
### Interpretation
- It is a measure of central location of the random variable
