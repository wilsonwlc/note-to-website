### Concept
- Aka **beta error**
- *What is it:* the probability of failing to reject the $H_{0}$ when it is false
- *What it does:* assess the reliability of the **hypothesis testing**
- *Notation:* $\beta$
- *Which:* False negative
### Definition
![[Type I and II error - concept 2.png]]
$$
\beta = P(\text{Accept } H_0 \mid H_1 \text{ true})
$$
