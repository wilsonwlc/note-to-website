### Definition
The **probability mass function (pmf)** of a discrete random variable $X$ is defined as 
$$
p(x)=P(X=x)
$$
- $X(\Omega)$ is the countable set of possible values of $X$
- $x \in X(\Omega)$
### Condition
1. $p(x) \geq 0$ for $x \in X(\Omega)$
2. $\sum_{x \in X(\Omega)} p(x) = 1$
3. $P(X \in A) = \sum_{x \in A} p(x)$ where $A \subset X(\Omega)$
