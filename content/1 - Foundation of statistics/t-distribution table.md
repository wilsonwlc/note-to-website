### Concept
- *What it does:* give the [[Critical value|critical values]] under a [[t-distribution|t-distribution]] curve according to a given tail area
- *How to read it:*
	- Row: degree of freedom, $r$ or $\nu$
	- Column: upper-tail probability (right-tail probability) or significance level, $\alpha$
### Practice
- Commonly tabulated upper-tail probabilities include 0.10, 0.05, 0.025, 0.01, and 0.005, with the exact columns varying between tables
