### Definition
A random variable $X$ defined on the sample space $\Omega$ is called a **discrete random variable** if $X(\Omega) = \{X(\omega):\omega \in \Omega\}$ is **countable**
- *Recall:* A mathematical set is **countable** if either it is finite or it can be put in one to one correspondence with the set of natural numbers
