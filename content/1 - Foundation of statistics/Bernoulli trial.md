### Notation
| Possible outcome | $X$ | Probability |
| ---------------- | --- | ----------- |
| Success          | 1   | $p$         |
| Failure          | 0   | $1-p$       |
- $P(X=1) = p$
- $P(X=0) = 1-p$
### Concept
- *What is it:* a random experiment with exactly two possible outcomes (commonly called “success” and “failure”), where the probability of success is $p$
