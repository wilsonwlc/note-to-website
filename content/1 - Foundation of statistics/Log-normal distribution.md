### Definition
If $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^{2})$ and $Y=\exp(X)$, then $Y$ follow **log-normal distribution**
It is denoted as $Y \sim \text{Lognormal}(\mu, \sigma^{2})$
### Property
- **pdf**: $f(y) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma y} \exp\left[-\frac{(\log y - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right]$, where $\log$ is the natural logarithm
### When 
- Model multiplicative product of random quantities such as growth factor on stock investment
