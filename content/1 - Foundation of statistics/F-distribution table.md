### Concept
- *What it does:* give the [[Critical value|critical value]] under an [[1 - Foundation of statistics/F-distribution|F-distribution]] curve according to a given right tail area
- *How to read it:* 
	- Usually printed as a series of two-way arrays (one for each significance level, *e.g.* $\alpha=0.05, 0.01, 0.001$)
	- Rows: denominator degrees of freedom, $r_2​$
	- Columns: numerator degrees of freedom, $r_{1}$
	- Each entry is the critical value $F_{\alpha, r_{1}, r_{2}}$
### Practice
- The three numbers are critical values corresponding to the commonly used tail areas 0.05. 0.01 and 0.001
- In some table, the last row shows the [[Percentile|percentiles]] corresponding to a [[Normal distribution|standard normal distribution]]. *Reason:* As $r \rightarrow \infty$, $t_{r}$ converges to $N(0,1)$
### Caution
- Check the table heading carefully, because the orientation of numerator and denominator degrees of freedom can vary between sources
