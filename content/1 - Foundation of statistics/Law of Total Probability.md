### Definition
#### Two events
If $0<P(B)<1$, then for any $A$ $$P(A) = P \left( A \mid B \right) P(B) + P \left( A \mid B^{c} \right) P \left( B^{c} \right)$$
#### General case
If $B_{1}, B_{2},..., B_{k}$ are [[Event & occurrence#Properties|mutually exclusive and exhaustive events]] *i.e.* a **partition** of the sample space, then for any event $A$, $$P(A) = \sum_{j=1}^{k} P \left( A \mid B_{j} \right) P \left( B_{j} \right)$$
