### Definition
Let $X$ be a random variable and $b \in \mathbb{R}$
Suppose $x_{1},..., x_{n}$ be iid realisations samples from $X$
- The r-th **sample moment** of $X$ is $$\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i={1}}^{n} x_{i}^{r}$$
- The r-th sample moment of $X$ about $b$ is $$\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{i=1}^{n} (x_{i}-b)^{r}$$
### Special case
- The [[Sample mean|sample mean]] is the first sample moment
- The [[Sample variance|sample variance]] is the second sample moment about the sample mean
