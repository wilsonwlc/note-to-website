### Concept
- *What it does:* Define [[Probability|probability]]
### Axioms
A probability on the [[Event & occurrence#Definition|sample space]] $\Omega$ is an assignment of a value, $P(E)$ say, to each [[Event & occurrence#Definition|event]] $E$ such that
1. $P(E) \geq 0$ for any event $E$
2. $P(\Omega) = 1$
3. For any sequence of [[Event & occurrence#Properties|mutually exclusive events]] $E_{1}, E_{2},...$ , $$P \left( \bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty} E_{i} \right) = \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} P \left( E_{i} \right) $$
### Practice
- If the third axiom is satisfied, $P(\cdot)$ is said to be **countably additive**
