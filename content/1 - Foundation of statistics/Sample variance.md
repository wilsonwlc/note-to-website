### Definition
$$
S^{2} = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_{i} - \overline{X})^{2}
$$
### Property
Assume $X_{i} \stackrel{iid}{\sim} N(\mu, \sigma^{2})$. By [[Sampling distributions from normal random sample]]
- [[Population mean|Mean]]: $E(S^{2}) = \sigma^{2}$
- [[Population variance|Variance]]: $\operatorname{Var}\left(S^{2}\right) = \frac{2 \sigma^{4}}{n - 1}$
- [[Population standard deviation|Standard deviation]] or [[Standard error|Standard error]]: $\sigma^{2}\sqrt{\frac{2}{n-1}}$
