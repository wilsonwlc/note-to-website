### Concept
- Aka **asymptotic distribution**
- *What is it:* the limiting distribution of a sequence of [[Random variable|random variable]] $X_{n}$ is the distribution of a random variable $X$ such that $X_{n}$ converges in distribution to $X$ as $n \rightarrow \infty$
### Definition
Let $X_{n}$ be a sequence of random variables with [[Cumulative distribution function (cdf)|cdf]] $F_{n}(x)$ and let $X$ be a random variable with cdf $F(x)$
If $\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} F_{n}(x)=F(x)$ at all continuity points of $F$, then $X_{n}$ converges in distribution to $X$ *i.e.* $X_{n} \xrightarrow{d} X$ 
The distribution of $X$ is called the **limiting distribution** of $X_{n}$
