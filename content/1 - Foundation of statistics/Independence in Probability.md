### Definition
#### Two events
Two [[Event & occurrence|events]] $A$ and $B$ are **independent** if and only if 
$$
P(A \cap B) = P(A) P(B)
$$
##### Alternative - Conditional probability
- If $P(A) > 0$, then $A$ and $B$ are **independent** if and only if $P(B|A) = P(B)$
#### General case
- The event $A_{1}$, $A_{2}$, ..., $A_{k}$ are **mutually independent** if and only if the probability of the intersection of any combination of them is equal to the product of the probabilities of the corresponding single events
