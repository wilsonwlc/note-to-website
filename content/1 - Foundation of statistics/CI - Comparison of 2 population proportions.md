### Formula
Suppose $n_{1}$ observations are drawn from a population with proportion $p_{1}$, giving a [[Sample proportion|sample proportion]] $\hat{p}_1$. Similarly, $n_2$ observations are drawn from another population with proportion $p_{2}$, giving a sample proportion $\hat{p}_2$
An approximate $100(1 - \alpha)\%$ [[Confidence interval (CI)|CI]] for $p_{1}-p_{2}$ $$(\hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2) \pm z_{\frac{\alpha}{2}} \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}_1(1 - \hat{p}_1)}{n_1} + \frac{\hat{p}_2(1 - \hat{p}_2)}{n_2}}$$
