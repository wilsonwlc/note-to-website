### Concept
- Aka **1st quartile**, $\frac{1}{4}$-**quantile**, **25th percentile**
- *Notation:* $Q_{1}$ or $P_{25}$
- *What is it:* The value below which 25% of the data falls
### Interpretation
- [[Median]] of the lower half of the dataset
### Calculation
#### Population
![[Quantile#Definition]]
#### Sample
- If the dataset has an odd number of observations, $Q1$ is the median of the first $\frac{n-1}{2}$ observations
- If the dataset has an even number of observations, $Q1$ is the median of the first $\frac{n}{2}$ observations
### Caution
- The data must first be ordered (sorted in ascending order)
- Alternative calculation methods exist
