### Concept
- *What is it:* the proportion of people possessing a clinical condition or outcome at a given point in time
- *How it is obtained:* usually measured by surveying a defined population
### Formula
$$
p_{v} = \mathrm{Pr}(Present)
$$

### When
- Determine the probability of disease given the result a test *i.e.* calculate [[Predicative value|predicative value]]. Prevalence is the prior information about the proportion of diseased cases in the population
