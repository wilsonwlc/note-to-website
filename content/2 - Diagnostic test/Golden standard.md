### Concept
- Aka **criterion standard**, **reference standard**
- *What:* An sounder indication about whether the disease is truly present or not. Usually it is the most accurate, but relatively elaborate, expensive, time consuming and even risky
- *When:* classify subjects into present/absent groups when establishing [[Sensitivity|sensitivity]] and [[Specificity|specificity]] of a [[Diagnostic test|diagnostic test]]
- *Caution:* An improper gold standard chosen would result in misleading measures of the test performance.
